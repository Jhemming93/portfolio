Scout the Elf

A holiday hidden-object game designed for short, timed challenges.

View Project

Project background

A popular physical holiday elf hunt encouraged associates to leave their departments to search, while cashiers had fewer opportunities to participate. I independently initiated a digital alternative intended to preserve the fun without disrupting customer service.

150 associates

Potential initial audience

1,200 associates

Direct development and hosting expenditure to date

Pre-launch

Management consideration and user testing; target launch December 12, 2026.

Player experience

Players sign in with a first name, last initial and store number. Daily timed challenges draw from image variations to discourage sharing answers. Incorrect selections incur time penalties and leaderboards compare performance.

Mobile-first iteration

Early testers reported difficulty seeing the character on smaller screens. I increased the character size and its selectable hitbox to make mobile interaction more forgiving.

Custom administration system

I designed a content management workflow that allows organizers to manage the experience without editing code.

  • Create and schedule daily challenge groups and image variations.
  • Upload backgrounds and character images, position characters and adjust hitboxes.
  • Manage player accounts, approved stores and administrator permissions.
  • Preview games, control demo and live availability, review feedback and monitor storage.
  • Configure bonus unlock codes and additional game content.

Development and infrastructure

Using an AI-assisted iterative development process, I defined requirements, directed feature changes and incorporated tester feedback. GitHub and Cloudflare were selected to minimize operating costs. The project has incurred no direct expenditure to date.

Stakeholder engagement

I reviewed policies concerning external applications and adapted the original store-themed concept into an independent brand. After presenting the project to a manager, assistant store managers brought it to the store manager for consideration. Management and HR personnel have participated in early gameplay. Formal approval remains pending; participation and potential audience figures are not launch results.

Beyond the daily challenge

The platform is expanding to include a digital colouring book, a Whac-A-Mole-style game and Spot the Difference. Administrative tools support configuring and previewing these activities.
